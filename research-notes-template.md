## Source quality rules
- Prefer current government, university/extension, standards body, exact-product manufacturer, or peer-reviewed sources.
- Require each source to support the exact claim, context, edition, model, population, or region stated; a related source is not a substitute.
- Use firsthand/community material only for clearly labeled experience or preference.
- For safety, legal, medical, financial, or location-dependent claims, use authoritative current guidance and state limits.
- Triangulate consequential claims when feasible. If suitable support is missing or sources conflict, remove the claim or state the uncertainty and limits plainly.
- Record access dates and archive or replace broken links before publication.
- Store only short quotations needed for verification; otherwise paraphrase and cite.

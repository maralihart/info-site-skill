## Page outline
1. **Hero:** audience/scope, promise, primary action, print link, core SVG.
2. **Essentials:** three rules/prerequisites and evidence-status note.
3. **How it works:** mental model and process/layer SVG.
4. **Steps:** 3–6 actions with observable checks.
5. **Choices (optional):** recommendation, alternatives, trade-offs.
6. **Troubleshooting:** diagnostic order, symptoms, escalation.
7. **Next steps and sources:** recap, reviewed date, references.
